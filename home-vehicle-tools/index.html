Formula and Calculation Method

Automotive and Household Formulas:

  • Trip Fuel Cost: (Trip Distance ÷ Vehicle MPG) × Fuel Price per Gallon
  • Fuel Economy (MPG): Miles Driven ÷ Gallons Consumed (or L/100km = [Liters × 100] ÷ Kilometers)
  • Tire Overall Diameter: [2 × (Section Width × Aspect Ratio ÷ 100) ÷ 25.4] + Wheel Rim Diameter in inches
  • Appliance Electricity Cost: [Watts × Hours Used per Day × Days ÷ 1,000] × Cost per kWh

Worked Example and Step-by-Step Calculation

Road Trip Fuel Cost Example (450 Miles, 28 MPG, $3.40 / Gallon)
Input Values:
  • Distance: 450 miles
  • Vehicle Economy: 28 MPG
  • Gas Price: $3.40 / gallon

1. Gallons required = 450 miles ÷ 28 MPG = 16.07 gallons

2. Total Fuel Cost = 16.07 gallons × $3.40 = $54.64

3. Cost per mile = $54.64 ÷ 450 = $0.121 per mile

Result: Total Fuel Cost: $54.64 (Consumes 16.1 Gallons | $0.12 per mile)

How to Use the Home and Vehicle Tools

  1. Select your tool tab: Fuel Cost (#fuel-cost), Mileage (#mileage), Tire Size (#tire-size), or Electricity Cost (#electricity-cost).
  2. For fuel: enter trip distance, vehicle fuel economy (MPG or L/100km), and fuel price per gallon or liter.

Common Mistakes, Limitations & Practical Guidance

  • Real-world vehicle fuel economy varies with aggressive driving, highway speed, vehicle cargo weight, and hilly topography.
  • Electric utility rates often include tiered billing or seasonal peak-hour surcharges.

Frequently Asked Questions About Home and Vehicle Tools

How do you convert between L/100km and US MPG?

The conversion formula is: MPG = 235.214 / (L/100km). For example, 8.0 L/100km equals 235.214 / 8.0 = 29.4 US MPG.

How do you calculate vehicle gas mileage (MPG)?

Fill your fuel tank completely and record your odometer mileage. Drive normally until you need gas again, then refill completely and note the exact gallons pumped. Divide the miles driven by the gallons pumped to find your true MPG.

How does tire size change speedometer accuracy?

If you install tires with a larger overall rolling diameter than factory specification, the tire covers more distance per revolution, causing your speedometer to read slower than your actual vehicle speed.

How much does it cost to run a space heater or air conditioner?

A standard 1,500-watt space heater running for 8 hours daily at an average electricity rate of $0.16/kWh costs: (1.5 kW × 8 hrs) × $0.16 = $1.92 per day, or approximately $57.60 per month.

How does fuel economy compare between MPG and L/100km?

In Miles Per Gallon (MPG), higher numbers are better (more distance per unit of fuel). In Liters per 100 Kilometers (L/100km), lower numbers are better (less fuel burned per fixed distance). To convert: L/100km = 235.215 ÷ MPG.
