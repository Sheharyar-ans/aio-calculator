EMI Calculator

An Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) is the fixed monthly payment made by a borrower to a lender each month. It repays principal and interest across the loan term, ensuring complete amortization by maturity. This calculator computes your exact monthly payment and total interest charges.

Formula and Calculation Method

The standard mathematical formula for calculating loan EMI is:

EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)^n] ÷ [(1 + r)^n - 1]

Where:

  • P = Principal loan amount borrowed.
  • r = Monthly interest rate (annual interest rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100).
  • n = Loan tenure in number of monthly instalments (years × 12).

Worked Example and Step-by-Step Calculation

Personal Loan Example ($25,000 at 8% for 3 Years)
Input Values:
  • Principal Amount (P): $25,000
  • Annual Interest Rate: 8.0%
  • Loan Term: 3 years (36 months)

1. Monthly interest rate (r) = 8.0 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.006667

2. Number of months (n) = 3 × 12 = 36

3. EMI = [25,000 × 0.006667 × (1.006667)^36] ÷ [(1.006667)^36 - 1]

4. EMI = [25,000 × 0.006667 × 1.270237] ÷ [0.270237] = $783.41

5. Total Payment = $783.41 × 36 = $28,202.76

6. Total Interest = $28,202.76 - $25,000 = $3,202.76

Result: $783.41 / month (Total Interest: $3,202.76)

Common Mistakes, Limitations & Practical Guidance

  • Does not include mandatory lender processing fees, stamp duty, or credit insurance premiums.
  • Assumes a fixed interest rate throughout the tenure; floating-rate loans will adjust payments when benchmark rates change.
  • Lenders may calculate interest on daily reducing balance or monthly reducing balance schedules, creating minor cent variations.

Frequently Asked Questions About EMI Calculator

What is the difference between flat interest and reducing interest on EMI?

Flat interest calculates interest on the full original principal for the entire loan life, whereas reducing balance interest charges interest only on the remaining unpaid principal each month. A 9% flat rate is equivalent to an effective reducing rate of approximately 16.5%.

What is an EMI calculator used for?

An EMI calculator determines your exact fixed monthly loan obligation before you apply for credit. It helps you verify affordability, compare competing lender interest rates, and adjust your loan tenure to fit your monthly household budget.

How does loan tenure affect my monthly EMI and total interest?

A longer loan tenure lowers your monthly payment because repayment is spread over more months. However, it significantly increases the total interest paid over the life of the loan. A shorter tenure increases monthly payments but saves substantial money in interest.

Can my EMI change during the loan repayment period?

Fixed-rate loan EMIs remain identical for the entire loan life unless you make prepayments. Floating or adjustable-rate loans will see their EMI or loan tenure adjust whenever the central bank or lender modifies their benchmark lending rate.

How is an EMI different from a simple interest payment?

Simple interest charges interest strictly on the initial principal. An EMI uses reducing-balance amortization, meaning each month interest is calculated only on the remaining unpaid principal balance while principal repayment gradually increases.
