Formula and Calculation Method

Auto loan payment calculation:

Taxable Price = Vehicle Price - Trade-in Value
Total Financed = Taxable Price + (Taxable Price × Sales Tax Rate) + Fees - Cash Down Payment
Monthly Payment = Total Financed × [r(1+r)^n] ÷ [(1+r)^n - 1]

Worked Example and Step-by-Step Calculation

New Vehicle Financing ($36,000 Price, $5,000 Trade-In, 60 Months at 6.8%)
Input Values:
  • Vehicle Purchase Price: $36,000
  • Trade-In Allowance: $5,000
  • Cash Down Payment: $3,000
  • Sales Tax Rate: 6.0% ($1,860 on $31,000 net)
  • Dealer / Title Fees: $640
  • Loan Term: 60 months (5 years)
  • Interest Rate: 6.8% APR

1. Total Amount Financed = ($36,000 - $5,000) + $1,860 tax + $640 fees - $3,000 down = $30,500

2. Monthly Payment on $30,500 at 6.8% for 60 mos = $601.07

3. Total Payments = $601.07 × 60 = $36,064.20

4. Total Financing Interest = $36,064.20 - $30,500 = $5,564.20

Result: $601.07 / month (Total Interest: $5,564.20 | Total Out-of-Pocket: $44,064.20)

Common Mistakes, Limitations & Practical Guidance

  • Taking an auto loan term longer than 60 months significantly increases the risk of negative equity (being "underwater" on your car loan).
  • Auto insurance premiums often increase with newer vehicles and must be factored into your total monthly driving budget.

Frequently Asked Questions About Auto Loan Calculator

What is the recommended car financing rule of thumb?

Financial planners recommend the "20/4/10 rule": Put at least 20% down, finance the vehicle for no more than 4 years (48 months), and keep total transportation costs (monthly payment, insurance, fuel) under 10% of gross monthly income.

How does trade-in equity reduce my sales tax?

In most U.S. states, trade-in credit reduces the taxable purchase price of the new vehicle. For example, trading in a $5,000 car on a $35,000 purchase means you only pay sales tax on the remaining $30,000 difference.

Is a 72-month or 84-month auto loan a good idea?

While 72-month and 84-month loans lower your monthly payment, they drastically inflate total interest paid and keep you in negative equity for years, meaning you owe more than the car is worth if it is totaled or traded in.

What interest rate can I expect on a car loan?

Auto loan APRs depend heavily on credit tier and whether the vehicle is new or used. Excellent credit borrowers typically secure 5% to 7% APR, while subprime borrowers may face rates of 12% to 18%+.

How does this tool differ from the general EMI Calculator?

This Auto Loan Calculator incorporates specific vehicle financing factors including trade-in equity allowances, state sales tax calculations, dealer registration documentation fees, and negative equity roll-in.
